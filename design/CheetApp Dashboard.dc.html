CHEETAPP
GT7 telemetry dashboard · Flutter / Dart

Live-simulated telemetry drives every panel below, so the RPM arc, pedal traces and fuel maths all behave as they would on track. Start screen and setup flow are clickable end to end.

Assumptions
  • 2340×1080 phone landscape is the reference grid; everything scales by ratio, not breakpoint.
  • Driver glances for ~0.3s — one value per card, no nesting.
  • Strategy numbers are slow-tier; RPM/pedals are fast-tier.
Maps to your architecture
  • Tach arc + pedal trace = two CustomPainter surfaces.
  • Trace holds a 150-sample ring buffer (5s @ 30Hz).
  • The two dashboard screens share one TelemetryState; a portrait layout would too.
