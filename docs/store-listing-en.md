## Full description
```
Cheetah Live is a companion app for drivers of the Cheetah SimRacing league (Gran Turismo 7 / Assetto Corsa Competizione).

Log in with your league-registered Discord account and see live telemetry from your GT7 console in real time while you drive:

• RPM gauge with gear-shift alert zones
• Current gear and speed
• Live lap times
• Tire status
• Fuel level with a low-fuel warning

Pick an event from the league's event list to join its live-timing session — your telemetry can also appear on the event's stream overlay if the organizer is broadcasting the race.

The dashboard adapts to your phone's orientation automatically — landscape mode switches to fullscreen so nothing covers the gauges.

Requirements:
- A Discord account registered with Cheetah SimRacing
- A PlayStation console running Gran Turismo 7 on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone

More about the league: cheetah.wificorp.pl
```
